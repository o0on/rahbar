# Library Services Guide 2026

## 1. Opening hours
The main library is open from 08:00 to 22:00 on weekdays and from 09:00 to 18:00 on Saturdays. It is closed on public holidays. During examinations the closing time is extended to 23:00.

## 2. Borrowing
Undergraduate students may borrow up to six books for fourteen days. Postgraduate students may borrow up to twelve books for thirty days. Books can be renewed twice unless another student has placed a hold.

## 3. Digital resources
Students can access the electronic journal collection through the library proxy server using their university credentials. Offline copies of licensed databases may not be shared outside the university network.

## 4. Study spaces
Group study rooms can be reserved for two hour slots through the library website. Silent study areas are marked on the floor plan near the entrance.
