# Examination Conduct Rules 2026

## 1. Before the examination
Students must arrive at least fifteen minutes before the start time and present a valid university identity card. Only pens, pencils and a silent calculator are allowed on the desk. Bags and mobile phones must be left at the designated area outside the hall.

## 2. During the examination
Talking to another student, using notes that are not permitted, or looking at another student's paper is misconduct. The invigilator may confiscate the paper and report the incident to the Examination Committee on the same day.

## 3. After the examination
Students must stop writing when the invigilator announces the end of the examination and remain seated until the papers are collected. Copies of the question paper may not be photographed or removed from the hall.
