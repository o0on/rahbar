# Student Services Guide 2026

## 1. Counselling and advising
Every enrolled student is assigned an academic adviser before the first week of term. Advisers approve course loads, explain degree requirements and sign withdrawal forms. Students may request a change of adviser once per semester through the student portal.

## 2. Health and wellbeing
The university clinic is open Monday to Friday from 09:00 to 17:00. Students can book a twenty minute appointment through the portal or by visiting the clinic desk. Emergencies are referred to the nearest hospital; the clinic does not provide overnight beds.

## 3. Complaints and appeals
A complaint about a service must be submitted in writing within fourteen days of the incident. The Student Services Office acknowledges the complaint within five working days and gives a written decision within twenty working days. Students may appeal the decision once to the Dean of Student Affairs.

## 4. Contact details
The Student Services Office is in the Admin Block, room G-12. The help desk answers questions about scholarships, housing and disability support during office hours.
