# Fee Refund Policy 2026

## 1. Withdrawal before the semester
A student who withdraws before the first day of classes receives a full refund of tuition fees, minus an administrative charge of five percent. Hostel fees are refunded in full when the room has not been occupied.

## 2. Withdrawal during the semester
Withdrawal within the first four weeks refunds seventy percent of tuition fees. Between five and eight weeks the refund is fifty percent. After eight weeks no tuition fee is refunded.

## 3. Course cancellation
If the university cancels a course, all fees paid for that course are refunded in full within thirty working days. Students are informed by email and the student portal.

## 4. Processing time
Approved refunds are transferred to the student's bank account within four working weeks. The university does not pay interest on refunded amounts.
