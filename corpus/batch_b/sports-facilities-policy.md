# Sports Facilities Policy 2026

## 1. Gymnasium
The gymnasium is open to all enrolled students and staff. Students must present a valid card at the entrance. Peak hours are from 17:00 to 20:00, when a thirty minute time limit applies to each machine.

## 2. Outdoor grounds
The football ground and tennis courts can be booked by student societies through the Sports Office. Bookings are confirmed two working days in advance. Unreserved use is allowed when no society booking exists.

## 3. Equipment
Equipment is issued against a university card and must be returned within twenty four hours. Lost equipment is charged at replacement cost. Damaged equipment must be reported to the desk immediately.
