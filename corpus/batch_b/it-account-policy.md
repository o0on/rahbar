# IT Account Policy 2026

## 1. Account activation
University accounts are activated when registration is complete. Students receive an activation link on their university email address and must set a password within seven days. Accounts that are not activated expire automatically.

## 2. Password rules
Passwords must contain at least twelve characters, including an uppercase letter, a lowercase letter, a digit and a symbol. Passwords expire every one hundred and eighty days. Sharing an account with another person is prohibited.

## 3. Multi factor authentication
Multi factor authentication is required for the student portal, the learning management system and the library proxy. Students who lose their device must contact the help desk to reset the second factor.

## 4. Closure
Accounts are closed one year after graduation. Email forwarded to an external address is disabled at closure.
