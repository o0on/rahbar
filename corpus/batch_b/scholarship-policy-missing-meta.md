# Scholarship Policy

## 1. Eligibility
A student with a cumulative GPA of at least 3.5 and no academic warning may apply for a merit scholarship. Need based scholarships require an income certificate issued in the current calendar year.

## 2. Application
Applications are submitted through the student portal before the deadline announced each semester. Incomplete applications are not considered. The scholarship committee publishes the award list within four weeks of the deadline.

## 3. Renewal
Scholarships are renewed each semester if the recipient keeps the required GPA and submits a progress report. A recipient who withdraws from a course without permission loses the scholarship for that semester.
