# Academic Rules Handbook — November 2026 edition

## 1. Academic warning
A student is placed on academic warning when the semester GPA falls below 2.0, or when more than six credit hours are graded F in one semester. The warning is recorded on the transcript and remains until the student returns to good standing.

## 2. Probation and dismissal
A student on academic warning who fails to raise the semester GPA to 2.0 in the following semester is placed on academic probation. A second consecutive semester below 2.0 leads to dismissal from the programme. The Faculty Council reviews every dismissal case.

## 3. Withdrawal
A student may withdraw from a course up to the end of the tenth week with a W grade. After the tenth week, withdrawal requires permission from the adviser and the head of department. Withdrawn courses do not count toward the GPA.

## 4. Grade appeal
A grade appeal must be filed within fifteen days of the publication of results. The appeal is reviewed by a committee that includes a faculty member who did not teach the course.
