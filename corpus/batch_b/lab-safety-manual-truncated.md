# Laboratory Safety Manual

## 1. General rules
Students must wear a lab coat, closed shoes and protective glasses in every teaching laboratory. Food and drinks are not allowed. Work alone is prohibited when a hazardous procedure is in progress.

## 2. Chemical storage
Acids and bases are stored in separate ventilated cabinets. Each container must carry a label with the date of opening and the name of the person responsible. The inventory is checked at the end of every month.

## 3. Waste disposal
Chemical waste is collected in colour coded containers. Solvents go into the red container and aqueous waste goes into the blue container. Sharp objects go into the puncture resistant box next to the sink.
